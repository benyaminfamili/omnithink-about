How It Works

Four steps. No tab-switching. No copy-pasting. No prompt engineering.

1
Ask your question

Type once. Pick which models should answer. Toggle Web Search for live internet context.

2
Answers stream live

All models run in parallel. Responses appear column by column in real time. Wait time = slowest model, not sum of all.

3
Compare & synthesise

Hit Compare for an AI-generated analysis of where models agree, disagree, and what to verify — with academic references included.

4
Debate & go deeper

Pick a format, review the proposed line-up, then watch models argue across up to 3 rounds with cited evidence. A blinded 3-judge jury scores it and hands back a structured verdict — who conceded what, and the strongest case on each side.

⚡

Parallel Streaming

All 5 models fire simultaneously. Wait time equals the slowest model, not the sum of all — drastically faster than switching tabs.

🔍

Compare

AI-synthesised analysis of agreements, disagreements, and what to verify. Academic references included.

⚔️

Debate Mode

5 formats — Standard, Oxford, Courtroom, Red Team, Rap Battle — with a hard-assigned devil's advocate, grounded named personas, and a blinded jury scorecard. Edit the line-up before it starts, or turn on Rigorous Mode for a citation-checked debate.

⚖️

Verdict

One click, structured synthesis: a merged best answer, a 0–100 ranking of each model's response, factual disagreements flagged, a confidence score, and a checklist of what to verify yourself.

🧠

46 Answer Modes + 11 Personas

Steelman, First Principles, Confidence Rating, Chain of Thought, Assumptions Audit, and 41 more — plus 11 personas that reshape voice and depth — applied to every model at once. No prompt engineering needed.

🌐

Web Search

Toggle live internet context injection before sending to every model simultaneously. All models answer with up-to-date information.

🎭

Meta-Answer

Distil five answers into one best-of-all unified response — the consensus of all AI minds in a single, clean output.

📎

Attachments & Voice

Upload images, PDFs or files and ask about them — extracted once, sent as text to all five models at once. Or skip typing entirely and dictate your question with voice input.

📄

Export, Share & Debate Reel

PDF report, Markdown download, email to yourself, a public share link, a single-poster Infographic, or a server-rendered "Debate Reel" video — take your research anywhere, in whatever format fits.

🔒

Private Mode

Toggle off-history. Your conversation is never saved and never linked to you — fully ephemeral sessions for sensitive queries. Only anonymous usage totals are kept.
